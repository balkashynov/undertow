01 / FROM LOGS TO EXPLANATIONS

The interesting part
is between the lines.

One worker is joining its processing strategy. Another has already revoked its partitions. A third is waiting for work. Separately, the logs look normal. Together, they tell the story.

Undertow aligns those observations without pretending that an absence of logs proves a stuck consumer, or that a commit-path message proves a broker commit.

01

Follow the handoff

Trace partition assignments and revocations across consumer lanes. Click any event to inspect its exact source and line.

02

Separate fact from inference

Native logs, instrumented progress, unknown intervals. Evidence coverage stays visible throughout the investigation.

03

Make the incident portable

Export a pseudonymized case or a Mermaid sequence. The local CLI can create a complete offline HTML investigation.
